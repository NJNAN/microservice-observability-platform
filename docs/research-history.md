# Research provenance

This project grew from a microservice observability experiment environment.
Original source snapshots, raw traces, controlled change variants, commit replays,
functional oracle outputs and evaluation scripts remain in their original
`preexperiment*` and `cia_preexperiment*` directories. Existing root research
notes are unchanged. They are archival evidence, not the V1 product interface.

The trace fixes and fixed journey are reused by V1. Historical results are labeled
as Compose results and must not be presented as measurements of the new kind stack.
See `PROJECT_V1_AUDIT.md` for evidence paths and the replay exclusion.
